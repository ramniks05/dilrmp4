Add New District

Create a district and its district login. All fields are required. The login name is the district name, with the first letter uppercase, followed by the LGD Code. The default password is that login name plus @dolr (for example, ABC and 123 become username Abc123 and password Abc123@dolr).

Choose the district to edit. Its login and password stay the same.
3 to 100 characters. Letters and spaces only. Numbers and special characters are not allowed.
