Copyright Policy

Material featured on this website may be reproduced free of charge after taking proper permission by sending a mail to the Department of Land Resources. However, the material has to be reproduced accurately and not to be used in a derogatory manner or in a misleading context. Wherever the material is being published or issued to others, the source must be prominently acknowledged.

The permission to reproduce this material does not extend to any material which is identified as being copyright of a third party. Authorisation to reproduce such material must be obtained from the departments or copyright holders concerned.

Hyperlinking Policy

Links to external websites/portals

At many places in this website, you shall find links to other websites/portals. These links have been placed for your convenience. The Department of Land Resources is not responsible for the contents and reliability of the linked websites and does not necessarily endorse the views expressed in them. Mere presence of the link or its listing on this website should not be assumed as endorsement of any kind. We cannot guarantee that these links will work all the time and we have no control over availability of linked pages. Links that lead to non-government websites show a message before you leave this website, and links that open in a new window are identified as such.

Links to DILRMP-MIS by other websites/portals

We do not object to you linking directly to the information that is hosted on this website and no prior permission is required for the same. However, we would like you to inform us about any links provided to this website so that you can be informed of any changes or updates therein. Also, we do not permit our pages to be loaded into frames on your site. The pages belonging to this website must load into a newly opened browser window of the user.

Privacy Policy

This website does not automatically capture any specific personal information from you (like name, phone number or e-mail address) that allows us to identify you individually.

If the website requests you to provide personal information, you will be informed of the particular purposes for which the information is gathered and adequate security measures will be taken to protect your personal information. We do not sell or share any personally identifiable information volunteered on this website to any third party (public/private). Any information provided to this website will be protected from loss, misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure, alteration or destruction.

We gather certain information about the user, such as Internet Protocol (IP) address, domain name, browser type, operating system, the date and time of the visit and the pages visited. We make no attempt to link these addresses with the identity of individuals visiting our site unless an attempt to damage the site has been detected.

Cookies

A cookie is a piece of software code that an internet website sends to your browser when you access information at that site. This website uses a session cookie only to keep you signed in after login and to count visitors; it does not store any personal information. Accessibility preferences (text size and contrast) are saved only in your own browser.

Content Contribution, Moderation & Approval Policy (CMAP)

Content on this website is contributed by the Department of Land Resources and by the nodal officers of the States/UTs through role-based logins. Programme data is entered by authorised State, District and IGR users, and is moderated and approved by the respective State nodal officer before it is reflected in the national reports. Static content (notices, policies and information pages) is approved by the Web Information Manager (WIM) before publishing. Content is checked to ensure that it is free from offensive or discriminatory language.

Content Archival Policy (CAP)

Announcements, notices and circulars are displayed on the website for their period of validity. Outdated announcements are removed or moved to the archive after the validity date. Programme data is retained for reporting and comparison over time, and earlier reports remain available through the date-wise report features of the MIS.

Content Review Policy (CRP)

Content on this website is reviewed periodically. Programme data is reviewed by the States/UTs on a continuous basis as it is updated. Static content such as About Us, Contact Us and policies is reviewed at least once every six months, and immediately when there is any change in the information. The date of the last update/review is shown at the bottom of every page.

Website Monitoring Plan

The website is monitored to check its performance, functionality, broken links and traffic, and to review the feedback received from users. Monitoring covers availability, page load time on different browsers and networks, functional checks of reports and downloads, and broken links (internal and external). Issues identified are corrected promptly.

Contingency Management Plan

The website is hosted in the secure data centre of the National Informatics Centre. Regular backups of the website content and database are taken so that the website can be restored in the shortest possible time in case of any defacement, data corruption, hardware failure or natural disaster. Any security incident is reported to the NIC/CERT-In and handled as per their procedures.

Security Policy

The website is security audited before hosting in production. Access to data entry and administration functions is restricted to authorised users through role-based login with captcha, password policies and session controls. Users must not attempt to gain unauthorised access, upload malicious content or interfere with the working of the website. Any such attempt is logged and may be prosecuted under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Accessibility Statement

We are committed to ensuring that this website is accessible to all users irrespective of device in use, technology or ability. It has been built with the aim to provide maximum accessibility and usability to its visitors, following the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0) and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA. If you face any problem in accessing this website, please send us your feedback. See the Help page for accessibility features and screen reader information.
