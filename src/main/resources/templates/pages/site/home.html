About the programme

DILRMP 3.0, for 2026–27 to 2030–31, is the next phase of the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme. The first phase digitised records, maps and registration. This phase joins them into one parcel-centric Land Stack.

The Land Stack is a GIS-based public infrastructure. Each land parcel is given one geospatial identity: the geo-referenced cadastral map, linked with the Unique Land Parcel Identification Number (ULPIN, or Bhu-Aadhaar) and the Record of Rights. The records remain with the State or Union Territory that maintains them. DILRMP-MIS 4.0 is where that progress is reported.
